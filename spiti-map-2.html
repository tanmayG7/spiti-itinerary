Fuel Strategy

Ambala +10L can→ Rampur→ Kaza (world's highest pump)→ Mandi on return

The Kaza IndianOil pump is the only fuel for nearly 200 km in either direction, so top up there no matter what. Above Kaza, your reserve can is your lifeline.

Drive slow. Look up at night. Trust the reserve can.
